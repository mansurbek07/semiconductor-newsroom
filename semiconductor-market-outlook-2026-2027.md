# Global Semiconductor Market Outlook: 2026–2027

**Sources verified: October 14, 2026**

> **Editorial note:** The figures in this draft reflect the supplied revision and should be checked against the original WSTS Q2 2026 release and the Korean export report before publication.

## Five Key Facts

### 1. AI is driving semiconductor growth

WSTS’s Q2 2026 update projects the global semiconductor market will reach approximately **USD 1.655 trillion in 2026**, representing roughly **108% year-over-year growth**. This is a substantial increase from the May 2026 WSTS Spring forecast of USD 1.51 trillion, or 90% growth.

### 2. Memory chips are growing fastest

WSTS expects memory revenue to grow approximately **302% in 2026**, driven primarily by AI infrastructure, high-bandwidth memory (HBM), and accelerated computing. This revised growth outlook supersedes the May forecast of approximately 250%.

### 3. Strong growth has already appeared in 2026

The global semiconductor market reached approximately **USD 702 billion in the first half of 2026**, up 102% year over year. This represents reported market performance rather than a full-year projection.

### 4. South Korea is a major memory-chip supplier

From January to May 2026, South Korean DRAM exports increased **304% year over year**, while HBM-related composite-structure chip exports increased **153%**. These figures highlight the importance of Korea's memory semiconductor industry to the AI supply chain.

### 5. South Korea is expanding production capacity

South Korea has plans to expand wafer production capacity over the next five years to support anticipated AI and memory demand. This is an industrial capacity-expansion plan, separate from WSTS's global market forecast.

## Key Risks and Uncertainties

Despite the strong outlook, several risks could affect semiconductor market growth:

- **AI investment concentration:** A large share of demand depends on continued spending by major cloud and AI companies.
- **Supply-demand imbalances:** Rapid capacity expansion could eventually create oversupply in some product categories.
- **Memory price volatility:** Strong memory pricing may encourage additional production, potentially putting pressure on prices later.
- **Geopolitical and trade restrictions:** Export controls, tariffs, and supply-chain disruptions could affect production and international sales.
- **2027 growth uncertainty:** The exceptional growth projected for 2026 may be difficult to sustain, particularly if AI infrastructure investment slows.

These are potential risks, not predictions that a downturn will occur.

## Definitions

- **WSTS:** World Semiconductor Trade Statistics, an industry organization that publishes semiconductor market data and forecasts.
- **DRAM:** Dynamic random-access memory, widely used in computers, servers, and other electronic devices.
- **HBM:** High-bandwidth memory, a high-performance memory technology used in AI accelerators and advanced computing systems.
- **YoY:** Year over year, meaning a comparison with the same period in the previous year.

## Conclusion

The semiconductor industry is experiencing exceptional growth in 2026, driven by AI infrastructure, advanced computing, and strong memory demand. South Korea is well positioned to benefit from this trend through its memory-chip industry and capacity-expansion plans. However, investors and industry observers should monitor AI spending, memory pricing, capacity growth, and geopolitical developments as the market moves into 2027.
