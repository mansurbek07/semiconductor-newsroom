The AI infrastructure cycle

Semiconductors are entering a new scale of growth.

AI infrastructure, accelerated computing and high-bandwidth memory are reshaping the global chip market. Here are five facts to know—and the risks to watch as the industry moves toward 2027.

Five key facts

Demand, performance and production

01 / MARKET OUTLOOK

AI is driving semiconductor growth

WSTS’s Q2 2026 update projects the global semiconductor market will reach approximately USD 1.655 trillion in 2026, up roughly 108% year over year. That supersedes the May 2026 Spring forecast of USD 1.51 trillion and 90% growth.

Updated Q2 2026 forecast
02 / MEMORY

Memory chips are growing fastest

WSTS expects memory revenue to grow approximately 302% in 2026, driven by AI infrastructure, high-bandwidth memory (HBM) and accelerated computing. This supersedes the May forecast of about 250%.

03 / ACTUAL PERFORMANCE

Growth is already visible

The global semiconductor market reached approximately USD 702 billion in H1 2026, up 102% year over year. This is reported first-half performance, not a full-year projection.

04 / SOUTH KOREA

Exports show memory strength

From January to May 2026, South Korean DRAM exports increased 304% year over year, while HBM-related composite-structure chip exports increased 153%.

05 / CAPACITY

Production capacity is expanding

South Korea has plans to expand wafer production capacity over the next five years to support anticipated AI and memory demand. This is an industrial capacity plan, not a WSTS market forecast.

Risks to watch in 2027

Potential headwinds, not predictions

01 · AI investment concentration

A large share of demand depends on continued spending by major cloud and AI companies.

02 · Supply-demand imbalance

Rapid capacity expansion could eventually create oversupply in some product categories.

03 · Memory price volatility

High prices can incentivize more production, potentially putting pressure on prices later.

04 · Geopolitics and trade

Export controls, tariffs and supply-chain disruptions could affect production and international sales.

05 · Slower growth after a surge

The exceptional growth projected for 2026 may be difficult to sustain if AI infrastructure spending slows.

These risks describe uncertainties the industry should monitor. They do not mean a downturn is inevitable.

Quick definitions

Terms used in this brief

HBMHigh-bandwidth memory used in AI accelerators and advanced computing.

The takeaway

The semiconductor industry is experiencing exceptional growth in 2026, powered by AI infrastructure and memory demand. South Korea is positioned to benefit, but AI spending, memory pricing, capacity growth and geopolitical developments will be important signals to watch in 2027.
